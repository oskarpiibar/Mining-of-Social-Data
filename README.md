Retweet network: [retweet_network_nodes.csv](/E:/courses/mining of social data/lab 1/data/retweet_network_nodes.csv) stores account vertices and activity attributes; [retweet_network_edges.csv](/E:/courses/mining of social data/lab 1/data/retweet_network_edges.csv) stores directed retweet ties, counts, and dates.

Original tweets: [original_tweets.csv](/E:/courses/mining of social data/lab 1/data/original_tweets.csv) has one row per tweet, with retweets and quote tweets excluded. Replies are included.
